Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-07. Last updated 7 October 2026.

This explains what IMPKT collects when you use the IMPKT pilot app and your wraps, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it so that you know what you are agreeing to. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you accept.

The short version

  • We record how you train: your punches, and the movement data from your wraps.
  • We also hold the body measurements you give us: weight, height, reach, stance, glove weight and level.
  • We use it to run the pilot and to improve the wraps, including building software that recognises punches.
  • We do not sell it, we do not advertise with it, and nothing in the app tracks you.
  • You can see it, correct it, take a copy, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent at any time by emailing pilots@impktband.com.

1. Who is responsible for your data

IMPKT, the company behind impktband.com, decides why and how your data is used and is the controller of it. You can reach us at pilots@impktband.com about anything in this policy.

2. What we collect

Who you areYour name, your gym, and the serial of your pair of wraps. These come from the invitation we set up for you. Your activation code is kept on your phone and checked there.
Your measurementsThe answers you give on the profile screens: weight, height, reach (if you give it), stance, glove weight, level of experience and your preferred units.
Your trainingFor each drill you finish: which drill, when it started and ended, every punch (the time, which hand, the power and speed scores, and which punch the drill asked for), and how well the punches matched what was asked.
Movement dataThe raw readings from the sensors in your wraps while you train: acceleration, rotation and orientation, plus the wrap's battery level and firmware version.
Technical detailsThe phone and system version, your time zone, and the identifiers of your wraps.
Your agreementWhich version of this policy you accepted, and when.

What we do not collect: your location, contacts, photos or microphone, or any advertising identifier. There are no advertising or analytics tools in the app. The app uses Bluetooth only to talk to your wraps.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

To run the pilot: sign you in, show your results and keep your wraps workingIt is needed to give you the pilot you signed up to (UK and EU GDPR, Article 6(1)(b)).
To improve the wraps: punch detection, scoring and the software that recognises which punch you threw, and to study how people trainYour consent (Article 6(1)(a)). Your body measurements and your movement data can reveal things about your body and fitness. To be safe we treat them as health-related and rely on your explicit consent for them too (Article 9(2)(a)).
To keep the service secure and fix faultsOur legitimate interest in running a safe and reliable service (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object to this, see section 7.
To prove you agreed to this policyOur legal obligation to be able to show consent (Article 7(1)).

Agreeing to this policy is a condition of taking part, because collecting and studying this data is what the pilot is for. You are free to say no, and if you do, you will not be able to take part. You can withdraw your consent at any time. That ends your participation, and what we did with your data before you withdrew stays lawful.

We do not use your data to make automated decisions about you. The scores in the app describe a punch, and nothing is decided about you from them.

4. Who receives it

  • Supabase, which hosts our database in the European Union (Ireland). It processes your data for us under a data processing agreement and may not use it for its own purposes.
  • Apple, which delivers the app through TestFlight and the App Store. Apple has its own privacy practices for that, which we do not control.
  • The people at IMPKT who build and support the product.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We will not give it to anyone else unless the law requires us to.

5. Moving data outside your country

Our database is in the EU. If a service we use processes data outside the UK or the European Economic Area, we make sure there is a lawful safeguard in place, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data until you ask us to delete it, or for 24 months after the pilot ends, whichever comes first. We keep the record of your agreement for as long as we need to prove it. Recordings that have not yet been uploaded stay on your phone until they are sent or you clear them.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • know what we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have wrong or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • receive your data in a common, machine-readable format, and have it passed to someone else where that is possible;
  • object to our use of it where we rely on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these, email pilots@impktband.com. We will answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK or the authority in your own country, and you may do so without contacting us first.

8. On your phone

Your profile, activation code and any recordings waiting to be uploaded are stored inside the app on your phone. In the Profile tab, Clear my profile and code removes them from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything stored in it. Neither removes data we already hold, so ask us if you want that deleted too.

9. Security

Data travels between the app and our database over an encrypted connection, and access to the database is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Age

The app is for people aged 18 or over.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will give it a new version number and ask you to read and accept it again before you carry on.

12. Contact

Questions, requests and complaints: pilots@impktband.com.
