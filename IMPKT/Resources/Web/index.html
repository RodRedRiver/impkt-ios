      Your programme.

      One minute to test the whole thing works, then six drills of twelve minutes, one per session, in order, at the start while you are fresh. They are not a training plan. They are how the wraps learn to tell your punches apart. If you throw the wrong punch or lose the tempo, carry on rather than starting again: five mislabelled seconds cost us nothing and a restarted drill costs twelve minutes.

      Ask us anything

      Your profile lives in this browser and nothing is sent while you fill it in. When you finish a drill, that drill is uploaded to our database and to nowhere else, and it carries a copy of this: your name, your gym, your serial, what the drill asked for and every punch in it. That is the whole point of a pilot, and it is the only thing we send. Clear your browsing data and you will be asked for the profile again.
